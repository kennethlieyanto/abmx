`abmx` turns Gmail-labelled bank mail into Actual Budget transactions. A Gmail
label selects the parser, the parser extracts the date, amount, and payee, and
your account map sends the row to the right ledger account.

Parsers are plain Rhai scripts under `~/.config/abmx/parsers/`, so you can teach
abmx a new bank without rebuilding it. Anything a parser cannot prove stays out
of the ledger and is reported as a broken label instead of guessed.
